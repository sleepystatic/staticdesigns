# Static Designs — Cold Call Script

## Before the Call
- Look up the business's current website (or lack of one)
- Note 1-2 specific issues you can see: slow load, not mobile-friendly, outdated design, no Google presence
- Have their site open on your screen so you can reference it

---

## The Script

### Opening (10 seconds)
> "Hi, is this [Owner's Name]? Hey [Name], my name is Bryan with Static Designs — I'm a local web developer. I'm not trying to sell you anything today, I just had a quick question. Do you have 30 seconds?"

*Wait for response. If they say no, say: "No problem at all — is there a better time I could call back?" If yes, continue:*

### The Hook (15 seconds)

**If they have a website:**
> "I actually came across your website at [their domain] and I noticed a few things that might be costing you customers — specifically [pick one: your site takes about 6 seconds to load on mobile / your site isn't showing up well on Google / your site doesn't look great on phones]. I put together a quick audit on it — would you be open to me sending that over? It's completely free, no strings attached."

**If they don't have a website:**
> "I noticed you don't have a website set up yet, and I work specifically with small businesses to get them online without the headaches of WordPress or those cookie-cutter website builders. Would you be open to a quick conversation about what that could look like for [their business name]?"

### The Value Drop (10 seconds)
> "What I do is a little different from most web developers — I custom-code every site from scratch, so it loads fast, ranks well on Google, and actually brings in customers. I work directly with you one-on-one, no middlemen, and my pricing starts at $499."

### The Close (10 seconds)
> "I'd love to send you a free website evaluation — I'll look at your online presence, your competitors, and put together a quick action plan for you. All I'd need is your email. What's the best one to send that to?"

*If they give their email:*
> "Perfect, I'll have that over to you within 24 hours. And if you want to chat about it after, I'm happy to jump on a quick Zoom call or meet in person — whatever's easier for you. Thanks for your time, [Name]."

*If they're hesitant:*
> "Totally understand. Tell you what — check out some of my work at staticdesigns.dev and if anything catches your eye, my contact info is right there. Either way, I appreciate your time."

---

## Objection Handling

**"I already have a website."**
> "That's great! A lot of the businesses I work with do too. The reason I reached out is I noticed [specific issue]. My free evaluation would show you exactly where you're leaving money on the table — no obligation. Would it be worth 5 minutes to see that?"

**"I'm not interested."**
> "Totally fair. If you ever need anything web-related down the road, I'm at staticdesigns.dev. Have a great day, [Name]."

**"How much does it cost?"**
> "My sites start at $499, which includes custom design, mobile optimization, SEO, and hosting setup. Most of my clients are in the $500 to $1,500 range depending on what they need. But the first step is the free evaluation — that way you'd know exactly what you're getting before any money is involved."

**"I use Wix / Squarespace / WordPress."**
> "I hear that a lot. Those platforms are convenient, but they come with tradeoffs — slower load times, limited SEO, and your site ends up looking like everyone else's. The evaluation I'd send you would actually show you a side-by-side comparison of where your site sits now versus where it could be. Worth a look?"

**"Can you just send me an email?"**
> "Absolutely. What's the best email for you? I'll send over some examples of my work along with the free evaluation offer. No follow-up calls unless you want them."

**"I need to think about it."**
> "Of course. I'll send you my info so you have it when you're ready. And the free evaluation offer doesn't expire — whenever you want to take a look at it, just reach out."

---

## Key Principles
1. **Lead with their problem, not your service** — always reference something specific about their business
2. **The free evaluation is your foot in the door** — don't try to close a sale on the first call
3. **Respect their time** — keep it under 2 minutes, don't push if they say no
4. **Sound like a person, not a script** — these are guidelines, not lines to memorize
5. **Follow up** — send the evaluation email within 24 hours of every call where they gave you their email
