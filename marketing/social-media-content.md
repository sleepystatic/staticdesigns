## Platform-Specific Tips

**Instagram:** Use carousels for educational content (higher engagement than single images). Reels for behind-the-scenes and project walkthroughs. Stories for polls and quick tips.

**TikTok:** Talking head + screen share works best. Hook must be in the first 2 seconds. Keep videos 30-60 seconds. Use trending sounds when relevant but don't force it.

**LinkedIn:** Write like you're talking to another business owner, not posting a corporate update. Personal stories and insights outperform promotional posts. Comment on other people's posts to build visibility.

**X/Twitter:** Threads perform better than single tweets for educational content. Engage in conversations. Quote-tweet relevant business/tech discussions with your take.

**Facebook:** Share to local business groups (with permission). Longer posts perform well. Tag clients (with permission) in project showcases for reach.
